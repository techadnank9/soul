  • You wrote the email and you didn't send it.
  • Three weeks is long enough to start writing a story about it.
  • Going quiet when you are not credited.

It never says

  • It sounds like you're feeling frustrated.
  • That must have been so difficult.
  • Conflict avoidant.

You can see all of it, and empty any of it.

It holds

  • +Your words, as sent
  • +What you decided, and what happened
  • +Patterns you confirmed yourself
  • +A first name, if you give one

It does not hold

  • −The audio. Deleted the moment the words come back
  • −A surname or a birthdate
  • −Analytics or crash trackers
  • −Anything it cannot show you the source of
